## P1-16 — Lazy restore of completed torrents (fbt-p1-16-vi1)

Engine implementation and its real unit tests are ready for review. **The bead
is incomplete and must remain open:** this checkout has no `serve` command, v1
API adapter, ApiConn or subscriptions. The four specified daemon cases could

Branch: `ntm/fbittorrent/cod_4-p1-16-vi1`, based on `f710a1c`.

The engine now distinguishes `RestoreOutcome::Dormant`. A positive completion
timestamp and stop-on-complete policy bypass librqbit admission on startup.
Legacy `completion_on = -1` remains unknown. Dormant views use ledger history
and validated saved metainfo: `done`, `checked: false`, progress 1, no peer or
rate counters. File-list reads also work without a handle. Stop and seed-off
leave completed records dormant.

Typed engine operations `SetSeed`, `Recheck`, `SetFiles`, and `OpenTarget` have
real implementations. Seed and checks admit through librqbit with fastresume
disabled. Nonempty legacy file-priority selections share dormant admission.
Open-target computes the single-file path or multi-file folder from saved
metainfo and stats it without admission. Missing selected data on activation,
or a missing open target, creates `error` / `missing_files`; a structured
`InvalidTorrentState` is available for the future API adapter. Checks and
missing-data failures durably clear the old completion timestamp. The tests
exercise actual good and corrupt piece data, and restart after corruption.

No v1 capability or legacy transport route is advertised by these internal
typed operations. Lifecycle and instance-lock ownership were not changed.
