## P0-10 — Resume on a finished torrent tells the truth

Bead: `fbt-p0-10-xeq`. Branch: `ntm/fbittorrent/cod_2`. Base: `ef2de87`.

Starting a completed torrent with seeding off now returns the typed error
`already complete; seeding is off for this torrent`, with exit 1 and the hint
`fbittorrent seed <hash>`. The live and VPN-parked engine routes validate the
selection before changing intent. Bulk start skips these records and reports
`skipped: [{hash, reason: "complete_no_seed"}]`; the successful hashes and count
exclude them. Completed records with seeding enabled remain startable.

The TUI offers `(U) Seed` for the completed, stop-on-complete selection. The
widget hides that row's start control while keeping it available for incomplete
torrents and completed torrents with seeding enabled.
